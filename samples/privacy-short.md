# Privacy Policy — QuickPay (Sample, <20KB)

Synthetic short policy for ClauseCards / DPDP demo. Not legal advice.

QuickPay collects name, phone, UPI ID, location, and device information to provide payments.

**Data sharing**
- QuickPay may share personal data with third-party analytics and advertising partners for processing and marketing.
- Data processing addendum (DPA) is available on request (attempted mitigation for R15 — engine treats as pass tier 2 when present alongside sharing).

**Retention**
- Personal data retained for 5 years from last transaction, deleted thereafter on request.

**Breach notice**
- QuickPay will notify users of a data breach within 72 hours of discovery.

**Governing law / Disputes**
- Governed by laws of India, jurisdiction of Bengaluru courts. Disputes shall be resolved by arbitration in Bengaluru.

**Misc**
- Class action waiver: user waives right to participate in class proceedings.
- Tenure: service term is 12 months, renewable. Maintenance of account data is described; no onerous repair burden.

Rent/Revenue context stored as monthly processing volume Rs 45,000 — below TDS threshold; included to test M3 non-applicability.
Text deliberately avoids deposit/overstay language so R01/R04 abstain.
