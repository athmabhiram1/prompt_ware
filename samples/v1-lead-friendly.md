# Rental Agreement v1 — Lead-friendly (Sample)

Bengaluru residential lease, 01 Apr 2025, 11 months.

- Rent Rs 30,000 per month. Security deposit Rs 60,000 (2 months, at statutory cap — MTA Sec 11 compliant).
- Refund within 30 days of vacant possession with itemised statement.
- Overstay compensation at twice the monthly rent for first 60 days then four times thereafter (MTA Sec 23 compliant). Tenant must vacate on time.
- No auto-renewal; renewal by mutual written consent only.
- Maintenance shared equally; no blanket repair burden.
- Governed by laws of Karnataka, jurisdiction of Bengaluru Rent Authority.
- Aggregate liability capped at annual rent.
- TDS: 2% under Section 194-IB on rent above Rs 50,000/mo (not applicable here), Form 26QC within 30 days.
