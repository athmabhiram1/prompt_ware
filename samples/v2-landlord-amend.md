# Rental Agreement v2 — Landlord amendment (Sample)

Same premises, amended 15 Aug 2025. Deltas vs v1 intentionally trap-laden for compare demo.

- Rent Rs 30,000 per month. Security deposit Rs 3,00,000 (10 months — exceeds MTA Sec 11 cap; breach of Rs 2,40,000).
- Refund within 90 days (violates 30-day clock; contradiction C3).
- Overstay clause removed — silent on compensation (removes MTA Sec 23 alignment).
- Auto-renews for successive 2-year terms unless 60 days notice (R08 hit, risk-up).
- Landlord may amend terms at any time at sole discretion without prior notice (R09 unilateral amendment).
- Entire maintenance and all repairs are tenant's sole responsibility (R07 one-sided).
- Landlord may terminate with immediate effect without notice (R10).
- Tenant indemnifies landlord and holds harmless for any and all claims including third parties (R11 blanket).
- Data clause: landlord may share tenant personal data with third-party brokers for marketing (R15 sharing without DPA).
- TDS cited as 5% under Section 194-IB (outdated; R18 hit, should be 2% since 1-Oct-2024).
- Retention: no deletion clause stated (R16 missing — walkaway).
- Amount mismatch trap: deposit stated as Rs 3,00,000 in words but Rs 30,000 in schedule (C1 if engine cross-checks).
